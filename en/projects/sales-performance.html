Sales Performance Dashboard

Where does the company really make money?

01

The question

Revenue does not show where the company makes money. A region can sell a lot and generate little margin.

The goal: compare revenue, profit and margin by region, then identify the regions to back and the ones to examine.

02

The approach

  1. 1 · SQL Server

    Import and check

    Imported the sales file and quality-checked the 9,994 transactions.

  2. 2 · SQL Server

    Make types reliable

    Safely converted numeric fields imported as text (TRY_CONVERT).

  3. 3 · SQL

    Analyse

    Sales, profit and margin by region, category, product and customer segment.

  4. 4 · SQL

    Prepare reporting

    A dedicated view, dbo.vw_region_performance, as the dashboard’s single source.

  5. 5 · DAX

    Define the KPIs

    Revenue, profit, quantity and margin, calculated consistently.

  6. 6 · Power BI

    Communicate

    A dashboard filterable by region, readable by business users.

03

The dashboard

Regional view of sales, profit and quantities, with key takeaways written for a business reader.

04

What the data says

Results by region, sorted by margin

  • WestFirst in revenue and profit: the most profitable region.
  • CentralThird in sales ($501.24K), but the lowest margin: 7.92%.
05

Recommendation

West

Build on it

Rely on the levers of the most profitable region.

Central

Audit it

Examine discounts, costs and product mix before trying to grow sales there.

Scope: public Sample Superstore dataset, used as a simulated business case. Conclusions describe observed trends; they do not identify operational causes that are not in the source.
